# Demo guide

## Positioning

Present Agent Fleet Audit as a **self-initiated concept project and working deterministic governance demo** for a multi-agent production-readiness service. Do not describe it as a deployed client system, live security enforcement platform, compliance certification, or autonomous production agent fleet.

Recommended service statement:

> I audit and stabilize multi-agent systems so teams can trace decisions, control tool access and cost, and define a production-ready operating model.

## 60–90 second walkthrough

1. **Problem and promise (10 seconds).** Show the hero and explain that outputs are not enough; teams need the decision path.
2. **Failure patterns (10 seconds).** Scroll through ownership, handoff, context, policy, cost, and replay risks.
3. **Architecture (15 seconds).** Show the tenant, collaboration, auditability, and provider-neutral panels.
4. **Control plane (25 seconds).** Open `/app`, run the vendor review, and point out the event timeline, policy decision, approval state, budget, and audit findings.
5. **Evidence (10 seconds).** Export the demo report and state that all provenance is seeded demonstration data.
6. **Service close (10 seconds).** Offer a Fleet Diagnostic: architecture map, readiness score, ranked findings, and remediation roadmap.

## What the demo proves

- Ability to design a credible governance event contract.
- Understanding of multi-agent responsibilities, handoffs, policy gates, approvals, budgets, evidence, and replay.
- Ability to translate complex infrastructure into a polished buyer-facing interface.
- A concrete process for a paid diagnostic and stabilization engagement.

## What it does not prove

- Live orchestration of external models or specialist agents.
- Durable production storage or authenticated multi-tenancy.
- Successful penetration testing, regulatory certification, or production SLOs.
- Client outcomes or customer adoption.

## Upwork-safe presentation

- Label the portfolio item “Concept Project” or “Self-initiated product demo.”
- Keep all pre-contract communication and payment on Upwork.
- Use screenshots/video/PDF samples without email addresses, phone numbers, meeting links, or outside contact instructions.
- If sharing the live site, ask prospects to communicate through Upwork and consider an Upwork-specific deployment without mail links or the external diagnostic form.

## Suggested portfolio assets

- Hero and architecture screenshots.
- Dashboard before/after running the vendor workflow.
- Policy/approval close-up.
- One three-page sample report: executive score, findings/evidence, prioritized roadmap.
- A 60–90 second narrated video using the walkthrough above.
