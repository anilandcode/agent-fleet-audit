# Agent Fleet Audit visual asset brief

These four original images support the premium dark interface. They are concept art, not product UI, and intentionally contain no logos, words, people, or dashboard text.

| Asset | Section | Prompt direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hero-fleet-core.png` | Hero | A governed AI fleet expressed as a luminous evidence network with a calm dark left margin for copy. |
| `governance-space.png` | Operational clarity | A secure architectural governance environment in matte black stone, smoked glass, and restrained champagne light. |
| `evidence-vault.png` | Diagnostic intake | A protected evidence vault: translucent glass layers, a warm provenance path, and deep black space. |
| `control-layers.png` | Platform and closing | A nonliteral multi-agent control surface made of dark glass planes, constellation paths, and a coordination core. |

Shared direction: original cinematic enterprise editorial art; charcoal, graphite, muted olive, champagne, and ivory; refined texture and restrained bloom. Avoid literal UI panels, generic circuits, neon cyberpunk, saturated blue or purple, readable text, or third-party branding.
